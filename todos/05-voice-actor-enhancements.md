# Voice Actor Enhancements & Roadmap (Phase 4 & Beyond)

This document tracks upcoming feature enhancements, visualizations, and tools for voice actor profile pages (`/voice-actor/:id`).

---

## 📊 1. Career Analytics & Visualizations

- [ ] **Career Timeline / Activity Heatmap:** Add an interactive line/bar chart (using `vue-chartjs`) displaying roles dubbed per year/decade across the actor's career.
- [ ] **Top Voiced Original Actors Breakdown:** Pie/doughnut chart showing the most frequently dubbed foreign actors (e.g. _"Official voice for Leonardo DiCaprio (25 films), Matt Damon (18 films)"_).
- [ ] **Media Distribution Breakdown:** Visual split between Movies, TV Series, Anime/Animation, and Video Games.

---

## 🎛️ 2. Multi-Dimensional Faceted Filtering

- [ ] **Filter by Media Type:** Quick filter tabs/pills (`All`, `Movies`, `TV Shows`, `Anime`, `Video Games`).
- [ ] **Filter by Studio:** Filter works recorded at specific dubbing studios (_Dubbing Brothers, Deluxe, Cinephase, etc._).
- [ ] **Filter by Decade / Era:** Preset era chips (`2020s`, `2010s`, `2000s`, `1990s`, `Classics`).
- [ ] **Filter by Performance Type:** Filter by `Lead`, `Recurring`, `Singing`, `Additional Voices`.

---

## 🎙️ 3. Audio Demo Player & Voice Samples

- [ ] **Hero Section Audio Player:** Embed an audio sample/demo reel player on profiles of voice actors with available sample audio.
- [ ] **Character Voice Snippets:** Ability to play short iconic voice lines directly from individual work cards.

---

## 📑 4. Professional Export & Bookmarking

- [ ] **Export to PDF / CSV:** One-click generation of professional CV/filmography resumes for dubbing directors, talent agents, and voice actors.
- [ ] **User Role Bookmarking:** Allow logged-in users to save specific voice acting performances to custom lists.

---

## 🏷️ 5. Enhanced Rich Social Cards & Knowledge Graph

- [ ] **Dynamic OpenGraph Previews:** Generate OG share cards highlighting the actor's 3 most famous character portraits for Discord/Twitter links.
- [ ] **Enriched JSON-LD Structured Data:** Deepen `schema.org/Person` with Wikidata, Wikipedia, and external agency links for Google Rich Results.
